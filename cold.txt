For a proposal, I’d order these so the reviewer understands **the problem → your solution → why it is innovative → how you will execute it → cost and timing**.

### Recommended order

**1. Project Title – Your Solution (250 words)**
This is the main body of the proposal. Explain:

* What Zootech is proposing
* How the solution works
* What makes it different
* Who it is for
* What you expect to develop

This should be the strongest and most detailed section.

**2. Problem Statement (50 words)**
Clearly establish the agricultural problem your project is solving.

**3. Short Abstract (50 words)**
A concise summary of the entire project: **problem + solution + innovation + expected result**.

**4. Innovation Track – Expected Impact (50 words)**
Explain what is innovative about the solution and the practical impact it could create.

**5. Design Tools and Method (50 words)**
Explain how you will actually carry out the project: research, experimentation, prototyping, testing, data collection, technology, etc.

**6. Estimated Costs and Timeline (50 words)**
Briefly state:

* How much the project will cost
* Major cost areas
* Project duration
* Key milestones

### Final proposal flow

**Project Title & Solution**
↓
**Problem Statement**
↓
**Short Abstract**
↓
**Innovation & Expected Impact**
↓
**Design Tools & Method**
↓
**Estimated Costs & Timeline**

One thing I would change: **put the Abstract first in the actual document**, even though it is best written after the other sections.

So the final document would appear as:

> **Project Title**
> **Short Abstract**
> **Problem Statement**
> **Solution**
> **Innovation & Expected Impact**
> **Design Tools & Method**
> **Estimated Costs & Timeline**

That is the most natural structure for a reviewer reading the proposal from top to bottom.
